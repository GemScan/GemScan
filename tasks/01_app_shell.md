# Tasks — App Shell & Capacitor Bridge

> **Spec:** `specs/01_app_shell.md` | **Milestone:** M1 — App Shell & Capacitor Bridge | **Depends on:** M0

## Milestone Summary
M1 bootstraps the runnable application: a Next.js 14 App Router project wrapped in a Capacitor 6 iOS container, with the `GemmaPlugin` Capacitor bridge fully wired on both the Swift and TypeScript sides. By the end of M1, the app can be loaded in a browser in mock mode and on a physical device in native mode, streaming token-by-token inference results through the bridge. The golden fixture library and Zustand-backed state management are also established here as the foundation for all agent UI built in M3+.
